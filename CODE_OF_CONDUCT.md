# Code of Conduct

Octonary Robotics adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as the code of conduct for all of its repositories, discussions, issue trackers and community spaces.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, whatever someone's background or level of experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory language, sexualised content or personal attacks.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Put the safety of people first, especially when working with or discussing physical hardware.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 defines expected behaviour, unacceptable behaviour, scope and enforcement guidelines, and applies in full.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers at **[conduct@your-domain]**. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Maintainers who are involved in a report will recuse themselves from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate in response to a violation, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a private correction, a formal warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban from the community.
